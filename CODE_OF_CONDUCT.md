# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be professional. Be respectful. Focus on the work.

## Guidelines

- **Technical merit first.** Evaluate contributions on their quality, not on who submitted them.
- **Be constructive.** Critique ideas, not people. Offer solutions when pointing out problems.
- **No harassment.** Threats, slurs, personal attacks, and deliberate intimidation are not tolerated.
- **No spam.** Off-topic self-promotion and low-effort bot submissions will be removed.
- **Act in good faith.** Assume others are doing the same until proven otherwise.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues/PRs, and ban users who violate these guidelines. Decisions are final.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channels associated with this project.
